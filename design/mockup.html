VocabMaster

A phone-first flashcard app with a bold & playful soul — punchy grape & coral, big rounded type, satisfying swipes and little celebration moments. Every frame below is built from the shared token system documented at the bottom. Light frames follow your device theme; the last two frames are forced-dark to show both modes at once.

Type scale · Fredoka + Jakarta

Display 42 Heading 30 Title 22 Body 16 · Plus Jakarta Sans Small 14 / Caption 12

Fredoka (rounded, 500–700) for every word, number and heading. Plus Jakarta Sans for body, meta and definitions.

Shadows

Motion & easing

spring · cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)
Button taps, card pops, celebrations — overshoots for a satisfying bounce.
out · cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
Swipes, flips, page transitions — calm, decisive exits.
Durations: fast .16s (taps) · base .28s (cards) · slow .5s (celebration).

Signature touches

  • Flame streak — 3-stop gradient (gold→orange→pink) with a gentle flicker.
  • Swipe stamps — rotated outlined badges (GOT IT! / REVIEW) + tinted card overlay.
  • Progress rings — conic-gradient mastery per word; fills to a ✓ at 100%.
  • Confetti — CSS-only falling pieces + a radial burst on wins.
  • Squircle language — big soft radii + colored shadows on hero CTAs.
  • Blob illustrations — organic gradient shapes for empty states.

VocabMaster · phone-first (390px), one-handed reach with a floating tab bar and thumb-zone actions. All type meets WCAG AA on its background in both themes. No build step, no framework — vanilla HTML/CSS, Google Fonts the only external dependency. Swap the emoji glyphs for real SVG/illustration assets in production.
